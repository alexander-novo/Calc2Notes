Tip

Procedure for Trigonometric Substitution

  1. Draw a right triangle with appropriate side lengths and angle 𝜃 labeled. Write down sin(𝜃), cos(𝜃), and tan(𝜃), in terms of the side lengths.
  2. Write down the substitution for the variable 𝑥, calculate the differential d𝑥, and specify the selected values of 𝜃 for substitution.
  3. Substitute the trigonometric expression and the calculated differential into the integrand. Simplify the results algebraically.
  4. Integrate the trigonometric integral, keeping in mind the restrictions on the angle 𝜃 for reversibility.
  5. Use the right triangle to reverse the substitution in the integration result and convert it back to the original variable 𝑥. In particular, compositions of trig and inverse trig functions of 𝑥 should be avoided (final answers should be in fully simplified form).
